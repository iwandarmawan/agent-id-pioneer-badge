Agent ID Pioneer Badge

A free commemorative badge for Agent ID holders. One badge per holder, free of charge — you only pay your own network gas to mint it.

What this is — and what it is not

  • A collectible record of being an early Agent ID holder. Nothing more.
  • No monetary value, no returns, no rewards, no airdrop. The badge will never pay anything out.
  • Minting is free: the contract accepts no payment. The only cost is the network gas for your own mint transaction, which you approve yourself in your wallet.
  • Only wallets holding an Agent ID can mint, one badge each, max 1,000 badges ever.

Minting opens your wallet for one transaction: mint(), no payment attached. You review and confirm it yourself — nothing is signed automatically, and no other transfer is bundled with it.

How it works

  1. Connect your wallet (the one holding your Agent ID).
  2. The page reads your Agent ID balance on-chain. If it is above zero and you have not minted yet, you are eligible.
  3. Press “Mint my free badge” and confirm the transaction in your wallet. The badge appears in your wallet as an NFT.
